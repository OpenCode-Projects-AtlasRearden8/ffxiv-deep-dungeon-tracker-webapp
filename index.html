Deep Dungeon Tracker

Offline, local-first run tracker for the Palace of the Dead, Heaven-on-High, and Eureka Orthos. Everything is saved in your browser's localStorage; nothing leaves your device.

Start a New Run

Personal bests are computed from your saved runs: deepest floor ever reached, and your fastest time from run start to each boss-checkpoint floor (every 10 floors). If a run begins at a later floorset, that entry floor doesn't count as a new record.

Low-energy, ADHD-friendly pre-run checklist. Tick items off, edit them, or reset to the suggested defaults at any time.
